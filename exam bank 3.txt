第 1 題
Tom and Becky are two Realtors in a dispute. Tom doesn't want to go into arbitration, but Becky does. What will happen?
A) The dispute will go into arbitration.
B) The dispute will not go into arbitration.
C) The dispute will go into mediation.
D) Lawsuits can now be filed.
正確答案：A
解析： If all of the people in a dispute choose not to mediate or arbitrate, they can advise the Board of Realtors in writing and be freed from the obligation to mediate or arbitrate. Note that everyone has to sign off. If just one party doesn't want to arbitrate, that doesn't count.

LaToye, who lives in Los Angeles, lists her home "as is." What does this mean in terms of property disclosures?
A) LaToye still must complete a transfer disclosure statement.
B) LaToye is not obligated to disclose known defects, but she can if she chooses to.
C) LaToye must not disclose known defects.
D) LaToye is only obligated to disclose hidden defects not easily accessible to the eye.
正確答案：A
解析： A common misconception among sellers is that if they list the property “as is” then they are not obligated to make disclosures of known defects. 第 17 題
Dudley Center is a commercial building. It has: A PGI of $923,445; A vacancy rate of 4%; $14,230 in other income annually; Annual operating expenses of $22,410; A cap rate of 6.3%. Given all of this, what is the value of Dudley Center, using the income approach?
A) $13,941,701.59
B) $36,937.80
C) $900,737.20
D) $878,327.20
正確答案：A
解析： First calculate the vacancy cost using the vacancy rate and the PGI: $923,445 x 0.04 = $36,937.80 Next, subtract the vacancy cost and add the other income to the PGI to find the EGI: $923,445 - $36,937.80 + $14,230 = $900,737.20. Then find the NOI by subtracting the operating expenses from the EGI: $900,737.20 - $22,410 = $878,327.20. Finally, use the IRV formula (divide the NOI by the cap rate as a decimal) to find the value: $878,327.20 ÷ 0.063 = $13,941,701.59.

Question: Michael, a California REALTOR®, has signed a listing agreement with Thomas, a seller. Michael knows that he will get compensation if the house sells, regardless of who brings the buyer. What type of agreement can we say that Thomas has? (加州經紀人 Michael 與賣方 Thomas 簽署了委託銷售合約。Michael 知道無論最終是誰帶來的買家，只要房子售出，他都能獲得佣金。Thomas 簽署的是哪種合約？)
[Correct Answer] exclusive authorization and right-to-sell listing agreement (專屬授權與獨家銷售合約)
exclusive agency agreement
open listing agreement
net listing agreement
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： Exclusive Authorization and Right-to-Sell Listing 是對經紀人保障最高的合約類型。在合約有效期內，不論是誰（甚至是賣方自己）找到買家，簽約的經紀人都有權獲得佣金。這在加州是最常用的標準委託銷售契約。
English Analysis: An exclusive authorization and right-to-sell listing agreement guarantees the designated broker a commission if the property is sold during the listing term, regardless of who procures the buyer (even if the owner sells it independently).
「專屬授權與獨家銷售合約 (Exclusive Authorization and Right-to-Sell Listing)」，其邏輯是：最高的保障： 這是對經紀人 Michael 保障最高的合約類型。不論是誰： 在合約有效期內，不論是誰找到買家，簽約的經紀人都有權獲得佣金。賣方亦同： 即使是賣方 Thomas 自己找到買家獨立售出房屋，經紀人 Michael 依然可以獲得全額佣金。
獨家代理合約 (Exclusive Agency Agreement)： 經紀人是唯一的代理人，但如果賣方自己找到買家，經紀人則無法獲得佣金。
普通委託合約 (Open Listing Agreement)： 賣方可以委託多位經紀人，只有「促成交易的導火線 (procuring cause)」才能獲得佣金；賣方自己賣出則不用付佣金。
淨委託合約 (Net Listing Agreement)： 佣金是超出賣方設定底價的溢價部分。

06. 重要事實披露義務 (Disclosure of Material Facts)
Question: Which of the following is the MOST accurate regarding the requirement to disclose material facts? (關於披露「重要事實」的要求，下列哪項敘述最準確？)
[Correct Answer] Disclosure of material facts is required because, if known, they could cause a buyer or seller to take a different course of action. (必須披露重要事實，因為如果已知這些事實，可能會使買方或賣方採取不同的行動或決策。)
By law, a seller must disclose what materials the property is comprised of.  (法律規定，賣方必須披露物業由哪些材料構成。)  錯誤點：披露範圍錯誤。  重要的是會影響買賣決策的「重要事實（Material Fact）」 
Disclosure of material facts is not required since "buyer beware" rules apply to real estate transactions. (不需要披露重要事實，因為房地產交易適用「買者自負」規則。)  錯誤點：法律原則適用錯誤。 雖然「買者自負（Caveat Emptor）」在某些地區或特定交易中存在，但對於「重要事實」的披露，法律是強制規定的。 
If a property is being sold "as-is," then disclosure of material facts is not required. (如果物業是「現況交屋」出售，則不需要披露重要事實。)  錯誤點：誤解「現況交屋」的效力。 法律規定，「現況交屋（As-Is）」只代表賣方不願意為維修埋單，但不能免除賣方對所有已知重大瑕疵（Material Defects）的法定披露責任。 
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 房地產法規對「重要事實（Material Fact）」的定義是：任何足以影響理性自然人做出購買決定、出價高低、或拒絕交易的房屋資訊（例如地基下陷、漏水、法定糾紛等）。法律強制規定必須披露，不能以「買者自負（Caveat Emptor）」或「現況交屋（As-Is）」為由隱瞞。
English Analysis: A material fact is any information that is substantially relevant to a party's decision-making process. If the disclosure of a fact could alter the buyer’s offer price or their decision to proceed with the purchase, it is legally deemed "material" and must be disclosed.
07. 賣方的瑕疵披露範圍 (Seller's Responsibility for Defects)
Question: Sellers are responsible for disclosing: (賣方有責任披露哪些房屋瑕疵？)
[Correct Answer] all defects on a property known to them (他們已知的所有物業瑕疵)
all defects on a property, regardless of whether the seller knows about them 
錯誤點： 這種要求超出了賣方的法律責任。賣方通常無需承擔對其完全不知情、且無法透過合理檢查發現的「未知瑕疵」的責任。如果賣方必須對所有瑕疵（無論是否知情）負責，這將構成對賣方極為不公平的「絕對責任（Absolute Liability）」 
only patent defects  
顯性瑕疵（Patent Defects）是指肉眼可見的瑕疵。如果只披露顯性瑕疵，而隱藏了賣方已知但買方看不見的重大隱性瑕疵（Latent Defects，例如牆後的漏水），則賣方和經紀人將承擔法律責任。 
only defects the buyer asks about 錯誤點： 賣方的披露義務是強制且主動的。 如果賣方或經紀人隱瞞了他們已知的重要瑕疵，即使買方沒有問，也構成不實陳述（Misrepresentation） 
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 賣方的披露責任以「已知（Known）」為界限。賣方必須誠實、完整地列出其所知道的所有顯性與隱性瑕疵，不能只披露買方主動詢問的部分。但賣方不需要對其完全不知情、且透過常規檢查也無法發現的未知瑕疵承擔無過失責任。
English Analysis: Sellers have a legal duty to disclose all known material defects to the buyer. They cannot withhold information just because a buyer doesn't ask. However, they are generally not liable for unknown or latent defects that they could not have reasonably discovered.

09. 供需關係與房價效應 (Supply and Demand Dynamics)
Question: A small town receives the news that a major employer will be opening a regional manufacturing center there soon. What housing market scenario describes the most likely effect? (一個小鎮收到消息，一家大型雇主即將在當地開設區域製造中心。哪種住房市場情境最能描述其可能產生的影響？)
[Correct Answer] Growth in population and the local economy will result in less supply and higher prices. (人口與地方經濟的增長將導致住房供應減少、價格上漲。)
There will be little effect on the supply or price of real estate from just one employer. 錯誤點： 如果該雇主是「大型 (major)」雇主，其帶來的就業機會會導致勞動人口淨流入，對小型城鎮的房地產市場產生顯著影響。

Because the announcement impacts industrial real estate, there will be little effect on residential properties. 錯誤點： 儘管工廠本身是工業地產，但工廠帶來的數千名工人需要居住，這會直接且劇烈地推高住宅房地產 (Residential Properties) 的需求和價格。 
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 這是標準的經濟學供需原理。大型製造中心進駐會帶來大量就業機會，吸引外來人口移入。在短期內住房供應量（Supply）無法立刻增加的情況下，需求（Demand）暴增會導致現有房源減少，進而推高房價與租金。
English Analysis: The influx of a major employer creates jobs, attracting workers and driving up the demand for housing. In the short term, because the supply of real estate is relatively inelastic (cannot be built instantly), high demand and limited supply will inevitably push property prices up.
10. 經紀人的共同侵權責任 (Agent Liability for Client Actions)

[Correct Answer] The seller-client asks the agent not to disclose a property defect and the agent complies. (賣方客戶要求經紀人不要披露物業瑕疵，而經紀人照辦了。)
Agents cannot be held liable for acting on any request made by their principal. (經紀人不能因遵從委託人的任何要求而被追究責任。) 
The client asks the agent not to sell to a specific marginalized group and the agent terminates the listing agreement. (客戶要求經紀人不要將房產出售給特定的邊緣群體，而經紀人終止了委託銷售合約。)

English Analysis: An agent’s fiduciary duty of obedience does not extend to performing unlawful acts. Concluding or assisting in the concealment of a material fact constitutes misrepresentation or fraud, making the agent personally liable alongside the principal.
法律知識點： 根據加州與聯邦公平住房法（Fair Housing Laws），前住戶患有愛滋病（AIDS）絕對不得揭露。因此最後一個選項中經紀人遵從客戶要求不揭露，是完全合法的，不會承擔責任。

23. 收益資本化法計算（IRV 經典年化陷阱題） (Income Approach & IRV Formula)
Question:
Dudley Center is a commercial building. It has: A PGI of $923,445; A vacancy rate of 4%; $14,230 in other income annually; Annual operating expenses of $22,410; A cap rate of 6.3%. Given all of this, what is the value of Dudley Center, using the income approach?
(Dudley Center 是一棟商業大樓，其數據如下：潛在總收益 (PGI) $923,445、空置率 4%、每年其他收益 $14,230、年營業費用 $22,410、資本化率 (Cap Rate) 6.3%。使用收益法計算，該大樓價值多少？)
[Correct Answer] $13,941,701.59
$36,937.80
$900,737.20
$878,327.20
詳細計算步驟 (Step-by-Step Calculation)
計算空置損失（Vacancy Cost）：
$$\text{Vacancy Cost} = \text{PGI} \times \text{Vacancy Rate} = \$923,445 \times 0.04 = \$36,937.80$$
計算有效總收益（EGI）：
$$\text{EGI} = \text{PGI} - \text{Vacancy Cost} + \text{Other Income} = \$923,445 - \$36,937.80 + \$14,230 = \$900,737.20$$
計算淨營業收益（NOI）：
$$\text{NOI} = \text{EGI} - \text{Operating Expenses} = \$900,737.20 - \$22,410 = \$878,327.20$$
利用 IRV 公式計算物業價值（Value）：
$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Cap Rate}} = \frac{\$878,327.20}{0.063} = \$13,941,701.59$$
English Summary: First deduct vacancy losses from PGI and add other income to arrive at EGI. Next, subtract annual operating expenses from EGI to find the Net Operating Income (NOI). Finally, apply the capitalization formula: $\text{Value} = \frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Cap Rate}}$.
PGI（潛在總收益） (Potential Gross Income) ： 100% 滿租狀態下，理論上的總租金收入。
EGI（有效總收益）(Effective Gross Income) ： PGI 扣除現實中不可避免的空置損失（Vacancy Cost），加上其他雜項收入（如停車費、洗衣費）後的實際收益。
NOI（淨營業收益）淨營業收益（Net Operating Income, NOI） ： EGI 再扣除所有年度營業費用（Operating Expenses），例如物業稅、保險費、管理費、水電費等。
 R：資本化率（Capitalization Rate, Cap Rate） Cap Rate 是指投資人在當前市場中，對同類型投資物業所期望獲得的無槓桿年度回報率。
邏輯： 這是從市場數據（最近同類物業的成交價和 NOI）中提煉出的數據。Cap Rate 越高，通常表示物業的風險越高，價值相對越低；反之，Cap Rate 越低，通常表示物業的穩定性好，價值越高。

Question:
An agent is doing a CMA on a two-bed, two-bath home. They have a comp that has two bedrooms and one bath that sold for $125,000. In this area, bedrooms are worth $6,000, and bathrooms are worth $3,000. What is the adjusted value of this comp?

詳細計算步驟 (Step-by-Step Valuation)

79. IRV 估價三角公式基礎計算 (Basic Cap Rate Calculation)

[Correct Answer] 5%
10%
20%
2%
詳細計算步驟 (Step-by-Step Calculation)
收益法核心三角公式 IRV：
$$\text{Income (NOI)} = \text{Rate (Cap Rate)} \times \text{Value}$$
題目要求解的是 Rate (Cap Rate)，因此公式移項為：
$$\text{Cap Rate} = \frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Property Value}}$$

English Analysis: Applying the foundational appraisal formula $\text{Rate} = \frac{\text{Income}}{\text{Value}}$, we divide the Net Operating Income ($25,000) by the transaction value ($500,000) to equal $0.05$, which translates directly to a 5% cap rate.

90. 雙邊合約的法律定義 (Definition of a Bilateral Contract)
Question:
Ashley and Leslie have entered into a written contract agreement where they both have promises to fulfill. Based on this information, you can infer that they have entered into what kind of contract?
(Ashley 和 Leslie 簽署了一份書面合約，合約中「雙方都承諾需要履行各自的義務」。根據這項資訊，你可以推斷他們成立了哪種合約？)
[Correct Answer] bilateral (雙邊合約)
unilateral
voidable
implied
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： * Bilateral Contract（雙邊合約）： 這是房地產買賣合約（Purchase Contract）最常見的形式。特點是「以承諾換取承諾（A promise for a promise）」。買方承諾付錢，賣方承諾交屋，兩邊都綁定義務。
Unilateral Contract（單邊合約）： 「以承諾換取行為（A promise for an act）」。例如懸賞廣告（你找到我的狗，我才付你錢，你在找到狗之前沒有任何義務）。
English Analysis: A bilateral contract is a mutual agreement where both signing parties exchange synchronous promises to perform corresponding actions (e.g., standard purchase agreements: seller promises to deed the home, buyer promises to wire money). Both sides are legally obligated to perform.
91. 職業道德與個人道德的界限區分 (Difference Between Ethics and Morals)
Question:
What is the difference between ethics and morals?
(「職業道德（Ethics）」與「個人道德/品行（Morals）」之間的本質區別是什麼？)
[Correct Answer] Ethics are determined by an external group and are standardized, while morals are held internally and vary by the individual. (職業道德是由外部團體或行業組織制定並具備標準化的；而個人道德則是個體內心所持有的價值體系，因人而異。)
Morals are determined by an external group and are standardized, while ethics are held internally and vary by the individual.
While morals dictate the bare minimum level of acceptable behavior, ethics dictate what is considered acceptable at a higher level.
While ethics dictate the bare minimum level of acceptable behavior, morals dictate what is considered acceptable at a higher level.
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 在加州房地產執照必考的職業道德章節中，這是非常經典的哲學定義區分：
Ethics（職業道德）： 是外部、客觀、標準化的。例如全美房地產經紀人協會（NAR）制定的《Code of Ethics》，所有會員不論個人喜好，都必須嚴格遵守這套統一的行業規章。
Morals（個人道德）： 是內部、主觀、因人而異的。是每個人基於宗教、成長背景所建立的內心善惡標準。
English Analysis: The primary differentiator hinges on the source. Ethics are code-driven behavioral systems established, mandated, and standardized by external groups or professional organizations (like the NAR). Morals represent subjective, internal guardrails rooted in personal or cultural definitions of right and wrong.
92. 潛在總收益（PGI）的空想理想狀態 (Definition of Potential Gross Income)
Question:
Which of these is the total rental income a property would receive if the property were 100% leased?
(下列哪一項是指當物業處於「百分之百完全出租（滿租）」狀態下，所能收到的理想最高租金總收入？)
[Correct Answer] potential gross income (潛在總收益 - PGI)
effective gross income
net operating income
yield
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： Potential Gross Income (PGI) 屬於估價收益法中的起點指標。它假設這棟商業公寓或大樓處於世界最完美的狀態：100% 的房間都租出去了，且完全沒有任何房客賴租或遲交。這是一個純理論上的最大產出值。
English Analysis: Potential Gross Income (PGI) is the absolute ceiling of a property's revenue-generating capability. It calculates the aggregate rent roll assuming zero percent vacancy and absolute collection efficiency across a 12-month calendar cycle.
93. 估價價值原則：退化原則 (The Principle of Regression in Appraisals)
Question:
A new $350,000 home is being built in a neighborhood of mostly $200,000 homes... its value is significantly less than the price. Which value principle of appraisal explains this?
(在一個周邊房屋平均價值僅為 $200,000 的老舊社區裡，新建了一棟造價 $350,000 的全新豪宅。雖然這棟新房裝潢最新、屋況最好，但它的評估價值卻顯著低於其造價成本。哪項房屋估價原則解釋了這個經濟現象？)
[Correct Answer] principle of regression (退化原則 / 貶值原則)
principle of progression
principle of change
principle of digression
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 這是加州考試必出的兩大鄰里環境效應原則：
Principle of Regression（退化原則）： 當一棟高品質、高價值的極品房屋，被蓋在一大群低價值、缺乏維護的破舊房屋中間時，周邊低階房屋的環境會把這棟豪宅的價值「向下拉扯」。
Principle of Progression（進化原則）： 相反地，如果你在一群 $1,000,000 的豪宅區裡擁有一棟 $500,000 的破房子，周邊的豪宅環境會把你的破房價值「向上帶動」。本題屬於前者，故選 Regression。
English Analysis: The principle of regression dictates that the worth of a premium property is adversely affected (pulled downward) by being surrounded by properties of significantly lower value or substandard quality.
94. 稅務評估價值的法規定義 (Property Tax Assessed Value)
Question:
What is a property's assessed value?
(什麼是物業的「評估價值（Assessed Value / 課稅評估價）」？)
[Correct Answer] the value placed on a property that is used in calculating property taxes (由政府主管機關為物業核定的價值，專門用於計算及課徵房地產稅)
the price a buyer agrees to pay and a seller agrees to accept for a property
the price for which a property will theoretically sell under typical conditions
the highest price an investor would be willing to pay for a property based on how well it will serve their investment goals
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 房地產有許多不同的價值定義。Assessed Value 是指當地的縣稅務估計官（County Assessor）根據地方稅法（在加州受到著名的第 13 號提案 Proposition 13 限制），為了計算你每年要交多少 Property Tax（房地產稅） 而專門核定出來的官方課稅基數。
English Analysis: Assessed value is the official dollar value assigned to a parcel of real estate by a local government municipality or county assessor specifically to calculate ad valorem real property tax bills.
95. 比較市場分析（CMA）挑選對比物件的最佳實務原則 (Selecting Ideal Comps for CMA)
Question:
Annie is performing a comparative market analysis for her subject property, a two-bedroom house in the Grand Point neighborhood. Which of the following would be the most appropriate comp(s)?
(Annie 正在為她的目標物業（位於 Grand Point 社區的一棟兩臥室房屋）進行比較市場分析（CMA）。下列哪一組對比物件（Comps）最為合適？)
[Correct Answer] two two-bedroom houses and one three-bedroom house in the Grand Point neighborhood that have all sold within the past six months (兩間位於 Grand Point 社區、且在過去 6 個月內售出的兩臥室房屋，以及一間位於同社區、過去 6 個月內售出的三臥室房屋)
four two-bedroom houses located in various neighborhoods of the city that have sold within the past week
one two-bedroom house that is more similar to the subject property than any of the other homes and has sold within the past year
two two-bedroom homes located 10-15 miles from the Grand Point neighborhood that have sold within the past month
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 挑選 CMA 對比物件（Comps）的核心黃金法則是：「必須在同一個社區鄰里內（Same neighborhood）」，並且「在過去 6 個月到 1 年內成交（Sold within past 6 months）」，數量「至少要有 3 間」。第一選項雖然有一間是三臥室，但它們都在同社區且在 6 個月內近期成交，可以透過加減價來調整，因此是四個選項中綜合評分最完美的組合。
English Analysis: The ideal composition of residential comps requires a minimum of three recently closed escrows within the same local micro-neighborhood that have transferred titles within the past 6 months. Proximity and timing take supreme precedence over slight bedroom variances, which can be mechanically adjusted.
96. 全美房地產經紀人協會對缺乏專業領域知識的揭露時機 (NAR Code of Ethics: Disclosing Lack of Expertise)
Question:
If a Realtor is asked to provide services but lacks expertise in the type of real estate involved, when must they disclose this lack of expertise to their client?
(如果房地產經紀人（Realtor）被請求提供某種服務，但她對該特定類型的房地產缺乏專業知識（例如住宅經紀人突然被委託去賣大型商業核電廠房或複雜農地），她必須在「何時」向客戶坦白披露自己缺乏這方面的專業能力？)
[Correct Answer] before offering services (在正式提供服務之前)
after providing services
at any time
Realtors do not have to disclose this information.
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 根據 NAR《職業道德準則》第 11 條（Article 11），經紀人不得提供超出其專業範疇的服務。如果你對這種類型的地產不熟，你必須在答應客戶、著手進行並提供任何實質服務「之前（Before）」，向客戶誠實說明。除非你找了該領域的專家共同協助並在合約中註明，否則盲目接案屬於嚴重的行業違規。

97. 保護期條款的損害防護效力 (Listing Agreement Protection Period)
Question:
You have a three-month listing period... Buyer Bob chooses not to buy the home. One week later, you learn that this home was sold to Bob directly from the seller to save commission... You could have legal recourse IF your listing agreement included:
(你與賣方簽有為期三個月的銷售合約。在合約最後一週，你帶了買家 Bob 看房。Bob 當時選擇不買。沒想到合約剛過期一週，你驚覺賣方和 Bob 私下跳過你直接成交了，目的是為了省下佣金。若你想透過法律途徑追回這筆佣金，你的委託銷售合約中「必須包含」下列哪項條款？)
[Correct Answer] a protection period (保護期條款 / 安全條款)
a backup offer
a retainer fee
a legal description
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： Protection Period（又稱 Safety Clause，保護期條款） 是用來防止賣方與買方聯合起來「過河拆橋」的致命護身符。條款規定：在合約到期後的特定天數內（例如 90 天），如果賣方把房子賣給了當初在合約期內由經紀人親自發掘、帶看並登記在案的潛在買家（如 Bob），賣方依然依法必須全額支付佣金給該經紀人。
English Analysis: A protection period clause (or safety clause) protects brokers from being cut out of a hard-earned commission. It states that if the listing expires, the broker is still owed a commission if the property is sold within a specified window of days to a buyer whom the broker originally introduced and documented during the active listing term.
98. 反報價的法律本質變更效力 (Legal Mechanism of a Counteroffer)
Question:
What is a counteroffer?
(什麼是「反報價（Counteroffer）」？)
[Correct Answer] a contract proposal submitted in response to a previous offer, modifying the terms of the original offer (因應前一份報價而提交的合約提案，旨在修改原始報價的條款內容)
a type of agreement in which the seller holds the title until the buyer has fully paid
a deposit made by a real estate buyer to show good faith
a contract whose terms have been completely fulfilled
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 在合約法中，Counteroffer（反報價） 具有雙重法律殺傷力：
它在法律上「直接徹底殺死了（Rejects/Terminates）」原始的報價。原始報價從此不復存在，買方想反悔回去答應都不行。
它同時扮演了一個全新的報價（New Offer），調換了買賣雙方的角色（此時發出 Counteroffer 的賣方變成了 Offeror）。
English Analysis: A counteroffer is a dynamic legal instrument that simultaneously acts as a complete rejection of the original incoming offer and the generation of a brand-new proposal with revised terms. Once issued, the initial offer is legally dead and cannot be revived or accepted later.

100. 收益資本化法簡化計算公式與大結局回顧 (Review of Cap Rate Calculation)

A building sold for $500,000 and has a net operating income of $25,000. What was the cap rate of the sale? (Hint: "Ask IRV.")
(一棟大樓以 $500,000 售出，其淨營業收益（NOI）為 $25,000。請計算該交易的資本化率（Cap Rate）是多少？)
[Correct Answer] 5%
10%
20%
2%
詳細解析 (Detailed Analysis)
中文解析： 題庫的最後一題再度以估價靈魂 IRV 三角公式 作為大結局的總結收尾。正如第 79 題的精準推導，用物業的年純利潤除以物業總價值，即可得出該地產投資的資本化回報率：

English Analysis: This terminal question reinforces the paramountcy of income capitalization mathematics. By applying the IRV appraisal formula, dividing the property's annual Net Operating Income ($25,000) by its market acquisition price ($500,000) yields a solid 5% capitalization rate.
