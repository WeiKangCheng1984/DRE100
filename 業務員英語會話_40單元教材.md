# 加州不動產業務員｜職場英語會話 40 單元

> **定位**：英語教學＋口語互動練習（非法務講義）。用語對齊《考前衝刺_280關鍵知識點》常見場景與術語，幫助業務員「聽得懂、說得出、換得開」。  
> **之後上站**：每單元可做成「看對話 → 聽發音 → 跟讀／角色扮演 → 抽換名詞練習」。  
> **免責**：對話為溝通練習用；正式交易請依現行加州法規、合約與公司政策，勿將此教材當法律意見。

## 使用說明（給學員／未來網站）

### 練習流程（建議 8–12 分鐘／單元）

1. **看中文情境** → 知道自己在哪個業務環節  
2. **聽／讀關鍵句** → 對照發音提示（IPA）  
3. **跟讀影子練習** → 先整句，再拆片語  
4. **雙人／自問自答** → A＝Agent，B＝Client／Seller／Buyer  
5. **抽換練習** → 只換 `{{槽位}}`，句型不變，舉一反三

# 單元正文

## Unit 01｜自我介紹與首次接觸
**Introducing Yourself**

**情境**：週年會／社區活動／首次見面，建立信任。  
**對應知識點**：專業形象；Agency 起點（Customer → 之後才可能變 Client）。

### 詞庫
`{{agent_name}}` · `{{brokerage}}`：[Summit Realty, Harbor Homes, Pacific Group] · `{{city}}`

### 關鍵句 ★
| 英文 | 發音提示 | 中文意思 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Hi, I'm {{agent_name}} with {{brokerage}}. | /haɪ aɪm … wɪð …/ | 你好，我是某某經紀人，任職於… |
| I help buyers and sellers in {{city}}. | /aɪ hɛlp ˈbaɪərz ənd ˈsɛlərz/ | 我在…協助買賣雙方 |
| May I ask what brings you here today? | /meɪ aɪ æsk …/ | 方便問今天什麼事找上門？ |

### 對話
```
A: Hi, I'm {{agent_name}} with {{brokerage}}. Nice to meet you.
B: Nice to meet you too. We're thinking about buying in {{city}}.
A: Great. I help buyers and sellers in {{city}}. May I ask what brings you here today?
B: We're just starting to look.
A: Perfect. No pressure—happy to answer questions and share what the market looks like.
```

### 口語練習
1. 用自己的名字換成 `{{agent_name}}` 說三遍開場。  
2. 對方說 “We're just browsing.” 回一句不施壓的歡迎。

### 抽換練習
把 `{{city}}` 換成三個不同城市；把 “buying” 改成 “selling” 再說一次。

## Unit 02｜電話詢問與預約看屋
**Phone Inquiry & Scheduling a Showing**

**情境**：買家來電問某房源；約看屋時間。  
**對應**：Showing；Ready, willing and able（之後才談）。

### 詞庫
`{{address}}`：[123 Oak Street, 88 Maple Ave, Unit 4B] · `{{time}}` · `{{property_type}}`

### 關鍵句 ★
| 英文 | 發音 | 中文 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Thanks for calling about {{address}}. | /θæŋks fər ˈkɔːlɪŋ/ | 謝謝來電詢問… |
| Are you available {{time}}? | /ər juː əˈveɪləbəl/ | …方便嗎？ |
| I'll text you the gate code and parking tips. | /ɡeɪt koʊd/ | 我會簡訊門禁碼與停車提醒 |

### 對話
```
A: Thanks for calling about the {{property_type}} at {{address}}. This is {{agent_name}}.
B: Hi. Can we see it this weekend?
A: Absolutely. Are you available {{time}}?
B: That works.
A: Great. I'll confirm and text you the address, gate code, and parking tips.
```

### 口語練習
對方說 “We're free after 2.” → 你提出兩個時段選項。

### 抽換
換三組 `{{address}}` + `{{time}}`；練習「確認＋簡訊預告」收尾。

## Unit 03｜釐清買家需求
**Qualifying Buyer Needs**

**情境**：第一次諮詢，釐清預算、區域、房型（不引導歧視條件）。  
**對應**：Demand／Utility；Situs（地利）；Budget。

### 詞庫
`{{beds}}` · `{{baths}}` · `{{budget}}`：[$700K–$800K, under $1M, around $1.2M] · `{{must_have}}`：[yard, home office, single story, good light]

### 關鍵句 ★
| 英文 | 發音 | 中文 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| What are your must-haves versus nice-to-haves? | /mʌst hævz/ | 必要條件 vs 加分項？ |
| What's a comfortable monthly payment range? | /ˈkʌmftərbəl/ | 舒服的月付區間？ |
| Which areas are on your short list? | /ʃɔːrt lɪst/ | 候選區域有哪些？ |

### 對話
```
A: To save you time, what are your must-haves versus nice-to-haves?
B: We need {{beds}} bedrooms and a {{must_have}}.
A: Got it. What's a comfortable price range—or monthly payment range—for you?
B: Around {{budget}}.
A: And which areas are on your short list in {{city}}?
B: Mostly {{neighborhood}}.
A: Perfect. I'll pull options that match those priorities.
```

### ⚠ 注意
不要問／不要假設種族、宗教、家庭組成「該住哪」；只談客戶**主動提出**的區域與生活需求。

### 抽換
換 `{{beds}}`、`{{must_have}}`、`{{budget}}` 各兩組，重講一次。

## Unit 06｜區位與社區（安全說法）
**Location & Neighborhood — Fair Housing Safe Talk**

**情境**：談交通、便利、生活機能；**不**談「適合哪種人」。  
**對應**：Situs；⚠ Steering／Implicit bias。

### 關鍵句 ★（安全）
- ★ This area is popular for its parks and commute options.
- ★ I can share school **rating websites** and public data—you can decide what matters to you.
- ★ What lifestyle features matter most to you—walkability, quiet streets, or shopping?

### ⚠ 禁止範例（練習「改寫」）
| 不說 | 改說 |
|:---|:---|
| This is a good area for families like yours. | Many buyers like the parks and weekend farmers market. |
| You might prefer that side of town. | Which side of town fits your commute better? |

### 對話
```
B: How's the neighborhood?
A: Location-wise, {{neighborhood}} is known for {{feature}}. What matters most for you day to day?
B: A quieter street and easier commute.
A: Then let's focus on homes that match those priorities. I can also point you to public data sources for schools and crime maps if you want to research.
```

### 口語練習
把任何「某種人適合」的句子改成「功能／數據／客戶自選」。

## Unit 08｜開價與市價白話
**Asking Price vs Market Value**

**對應**：Market value ≠ Market price；Listing price。

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ The **list price** is {{list_price}}—that's the asking price.
- ★ **Market value** is what a typical buyer would likely pay under normal conditions.
- ★ The final **sale price** is what the parties actually agree on.

### 對話
```
B: Is {{list_price}} a fair price?

```

```

## Unit 11｜解釋 Exclusive Right-to-Sell
**Exclusive Right-to-Sell (Authorization)**

**對應**：衝刺 #49 — 誰賣出都付佣（含屋主自售）。

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ With an exclusive right-to-sell listing, I'm your exclusive agent for the term.
- ★ If the property sells during the listing period, a commission is typically owed—even if you find the buyer yourself.
- ★ In return, I provide full marketing, showings coordination, and negotiation support.

### 對話
```
S: What if my cousin wants to buy?
A: Under an exclusive right-to-sell agreement, a commission is typically still owed if it sells during the listing term—even with a buyer you introduce. That's why this listing type lets me invest fully in marketing. We can review every clause before you sign.
```

### 口語練習
用「好處／義務」各兩句說明，避免施壓用語。

## Unit 12｜Exclusive Agency／Open 對照
**Exclusive Agency vs Open Listing**

**對應**：#50 Exclusive agency；#51 Open listing；#53 Procuring cause。

### 關鍵句 ★
| 類型 | 一句話 |
|:---|:---|
| Exclusive agency | One broker; if **you** sell it yourself, you may owe no commission. |
| Open listing | Multiple brokers; usually only the procuring-cause broker gets paid. |

### 對話
```
S: Can I still sell it myself?
A: With exclusive agency, if you sell it yourself, you often owe no commission—but you still work with one brokerage for agent-procured buyers. With an open listing, several brokers may market it, and payment usually depends on who is the procuring cause. I can compare pros and cons for your situation.
```

### 對話
```
A: I recommend a {{inspector}} for {{time}}. After the report, we'll review any {{issue}} items and decide: request repairs, credit, or proceed.
B: What if it's serious?
A: Then we use the inspection contingency timeline in your contract and respond in writing.
```

### 抽換
輪換 `{{issue}}`：roof／HVAC／plumbing／electrical，練習回報語氣（中性、不恐嚇）。

## Unit 21｜屋況瑕疵討論（安全說法）
**Talking About Defects Carefully**

**對應**：Material fact；Latent vs Patent；不得協助隱瞞。

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ Sellers must disclose known material facts; agents shouldn't hide them.
- ★ I'll point you to the disclosure package—please read it carefully.
- ★ For technical opinions, rely on qualified inspectors and specialists.

### 對話
```
B: There's a stain on the ceiling. Is that a big problem?
A: It's worth investigating. Let's note it for the inspector and review what the seller disclosed. I won't guess about hidden conditions—specialists should evaluate.
```

### ⚠
賣方若要求隱瞞重大瑕疵：拒絕配合，並依公司／法規處理（對應衝刺揭露倫理）。

## Unit 22｜As-is 溝通
**“As-Is” Without Myths**

**對應**：#90 As-is＝未必免揭露；加州仍常須 TDS／已知重大瑕疵。

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ “As-is” often means the seller prefers not to make repairs.
- ★ It does **not** mean “no disclosures.”
- ★ You still review disclosures and may negotiate within your contingency rights.

### 對話
```
S: We're selling as-is.
A: Understood—you prefer not to complete repairs. We'll still complete required disclosures for known material facts, and buyers will review those documents.
```

## Unit 23｜Pre-1978／Lead 提示用語
**Lead-Based Paint Disclosure Talk**

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ Homes built before 1978 may have lead-based paint.
- ★ Federal disclosure rules apply; buyers get the required pamphlets/forms.
- ★ If you have concerns, consider a qualified lead assessment.

## Unit 31｜投資客：Cap Rate 白話
**Talking Cap Rate with Investors**

**對應**：#129–136 NOI、Cap rate、IRV（口語版）。

### 關鍵句 ★
- ★ Cap rate is basically NOI divided by value.
- ★ Higher cap rate often means higher relative risk—or a lower price for the same income.
- ★ This is education, not a guarantee of returns.

### 對話
```
C: What's the cap rate?
A: Using estimated NOI and the asking price, the indicated cap rate is about {{cap_rate}}：[4.5%, 5%, 6%]. Let's verify income, vacancy, and expenses before you rely on that number.
```

### 抽換

```

## Unit 38｜公平住房安全用語特訓
**Fair Housing Language Gym** ⚠

**對應**：#105–109 Implicit bias、Steering、Blockbusting、Redlining。

### 原則
1. 談 **房產／生活機能／客戶自述需求**，不談「哪種人」  
2. 不預測鄰居組成、不恐嚇「社區變了快賣」  
3. 貸款與資格交給貸款專員＋統一標準  

### 改寫練習（必做）
| 危險說法 | 安全改寫 |
|:---|:---|
| You'll feel more comfortable in that area. | Which area matches your commute and budget best? |
| That neighborhood is changing—sell now. | If your goal is timing the market, we can review recent sales data together. |
| I know a lender who helps “your community.” | I can share lenders who work with many local buyers; apply with the same process. |

### 角色扮演
學員 A 故意說出危險句 → 學員 B 立刻改寫成安全句（限時 10 秒）。
